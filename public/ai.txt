## Statement

ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.

The 3D scans, thumbnails, photographs, annotations, and descriptive text
published on this site are not offered under any public license. No license to
reuse them is granted to any party, including visitors, search engines, AI
developers, and dataset compilers.

Archivists own their scans and retain copyright in them. They grant the archive
a sole, perpetual license to hold and display the scans on this site. Because
that grant is sole, no third party can obtain rights in these works from any
source: not from the archive, which does not sublicense, and not from the
Archivist, who has agreed not to license the same scan elsewhere.

The archive uses contributed scans on this site and nowhere else.

## Reservation of rights

Use of these works as training, fine-tuning, validation, evaluation, or
grounding data for machine learning models is prohibited and expressly
reserved. This includes generative models for 3D geometry, images, video, and
text, and includes retrieval-augmented generation over the archive's contents.

The reservation is made under Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 on
copyright in the Digital Single Market, and under equivalent
text-and-data-mining provisions in other jurisdictions.

The reservation applies regardless of how a copy was obtained, including
through third-party crawls, redistributed datasets, and cached copies.
Possession of a copy conveys no right to use it.

## Archive commitments

These bind the archive, not only its visitors. The archive does not and will
not:

- Sell, rent, or otherwise commercialise contributed scans.
- Transfer or assign them to another party unless these commitments transfer
  intact.
- Sublicense or redistribute them. There are no dataset releases, bulk exports,
  partner feeds, or download endpoints.
- Use them, or permit their use, as machine learning training data.

## Permitted without request

- Viewing and browsing the archive.
- Search indexing that returns links and short excerpts.
- Linking to, citing, and writing about the archive.

## Requires written permission

- Reuse, reproduction, or redistribution of scans or thumbnails.
- Derivative works built from scan geometry.
- Commercial use of any kind.
- Machine learning use as described above. Permission will not be granted.
