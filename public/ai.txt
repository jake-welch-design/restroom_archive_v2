## The archive's own commitments

These bind the archive, not just its visitors. The archive does not and will
not:

- Sell, rent, or otherwise commercialise contributed scans.
- Transfer or assign them to another party without these commitments carrying
  over intact.
- Sublicense or redistribute them -- no dataset releases, no bulk exports, no
  partner feeds, no download endpoint.
- Use them, or permit their use, as machine learning training data.

## Permitted without asking
